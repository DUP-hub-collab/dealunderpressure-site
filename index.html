THE MARGIN TEST

Six places a rental deal breaks. Check every one before you make an offer.

Most landlords find out they're losing money after they close. MARGIN catches it before.

M

Market rent ceiling

What comparable units actually rent for, not what you need them to rent for.

A

Assessed taxes & insurance

Model taxes at double. Mine went from $2,345 to $6,350.

R

Real collections

Underwrite 75–80% of scheduled rent. Never 100%.

G

Gaps in occupancy

Run it at 85%, 75% and 50%. Mine hit 40%.

I

Interest & financing

Know what your lender does when the numbers slip.

N

Necessary reserves

Six months of costs in the bank before you close.
