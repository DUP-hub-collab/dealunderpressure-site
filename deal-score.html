    How the score works

    Each MARGIN number is worth up to about 17 points: Market rent ceiling, Assessed taxes & insurance, Real collections, Gaps in occupancy, Interest & financing, and Necessary reserves. Taxes are tested at double the current bill, because mine went from $2,345 to $6,350 after the sale. Occupancy is scored by the break-even point: how full the building has to be just to cover the bills.

    It's a quick gut check, not an appraisal. A low score doesn't mean walk away. It means you have questions to answer before you sign.
